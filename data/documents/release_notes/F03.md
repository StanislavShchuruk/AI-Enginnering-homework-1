# PeopleFlow Release Notes v1.8

Released 2026-03-01. These notes cover the version used through 2026-08-31. PeopleFlow Release Notes v2.0 describe the changes introduced on 2026-09-01.

## Features available in v1.8
Employees could view their attendance timeline, submit annual leave requests, download published payslips, and upload employee documents. Managers could decide annual leave and overtime requests through the team dashboard. Session expiry was 30 minutes idle or 12 hours after login. Password lockout followed five failures in 15 minutes and lasted 30 minutes.

## Attendance in this release
Employees could not submit attendance correction requests. They sent the original times, proposed times, and reason to their manager. Managers directly adjusted unlocked records within 30 calendar days; older or locked periods required HR Payroll. Attendance Correction Guide v1 contains that historical procedure.

## Cancelling leave
Employees could submit leave themselves, but cancellation required contacting HR Support with the request reference and the requested action. HR recorded the cancellation and restored the balance. There was no employee Cancel leave control. Leave Request Guide v1 is the matching historical guide.

## Shift swaps
Swaps were manager-initiated. Employees asked the manager to arrange a trade; the manager checked both workers' agreement, team skills, rest, and hour limits, then reassigned both shifts. There was no employee Propose swap button or colleague acceptance timer. The resulting schedule still required 11 consecutive hours of rest.

## Upgrade boundary
The v2.0 cutover is 2026-09-01. New actions after cutover use current procedures even when they concern an earlier date, subject to payroll locks. Existing attendance events, leave balances, and published rosters are retained. PeopleFlow Release Notes v2.0 explains the new self-service requests, leave cancellation, and employee-initiated swap controls.
