# Support Escalation & Security Incidents

IT Support | Updated 1 September 2026

## Choose an escalation route
Use Help > Escalations > Service queue for ordinary unresolved support work. Use Help > Security incident for suspected unauthorized access, wrong-recipient exports, exposed medical files, or unexpected payroll downloads. These are role-based application queues, not personal contact details. If normal sign-in is unavailable, use the Security report option on the organization's approved PeopleFlow login page. That form accepts reports continuously.

## Immediate incident procedure
1. Stop the action that may be exposing information; do not repeat a bulk export to test it.
2. Record the first observed time with timezone, affected module, and a restricted record reference.
3. Report through the Security incident form and preserve the incident reference.
4. Follow containment instructions from the security response group.

Do not forward exposed records, copy them into public chat, delete audit evidence, or attempt to investigate another employee's account. If a shared link is involved, record its internal reference in the secure form rather than reposting the link. Security administrators may revoke sessions, disable the affected share, or temporarily restrict a role while preserving logs.

## Ownership and timing
The service queue lead handles missed first-response targets from Support Priority & SLA Policy. A High service incident without a workaround after four business hours escalates to the application owner. Suspected security incidents bypass that waiting period and route immediately to the security response group. Continuous reporting does not create a separate guaranteed out-of-hours response SLA. The security response group assigns severity, directs containment, and decides who may receive an incident update.

## Closure and communication
The incident owner records the affected scope, containment time, recovery checks, and remaining follow-up actions. HR Operations reviews any employee-facing explanation before it is distributed. Only authorized incident owners issue broad notifications. Closure requires confirmation that unauthorized access has stopped and the corrected permissions or configuration were checked. Retain relevant audit references under Employee Data Privacy Policy; an investigation hold pauses routine deletion for those records. An ordinary user should be told the safe next step without receiving unrelated confidential investigation details.
