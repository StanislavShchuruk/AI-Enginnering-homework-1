How to Submit a Support Ticket

IT Support | Updated 1 September 2026

Start in the right channel

Help > Submit ticket creates a PeopleFlow support record. Use a ticket for a reproducible application failure or an unexplained result after following the relevant manual. Leave approval, attendance approval, and payroll authorization remain separate business processes. Creating a ticket does not approve a request, stop a shift, or guarantee a payroll adjustment. An active data exposure should use the security incident route in Support Escalation & Security Incidents immediately.

Required information

  1. Select a category: Authentication, Absence, Attendance, Shifts, Payroll, Profile, or Technical.
  2. Enter a short subject describing the affected action and visible result.
  3. Describe expected behavior, actual behavior, and numbered reproduction steps.
  4. Add the event time with timezone, application version, browser version, and exact error code.
  5. Select impact and submit. Keep the assigned ticket reference for follow-up.

Priority is assessed by support under Support Priority & SLA Policy. Select High impact for an organization-wide outage, a payroll run blocked before cutoff, or suspected exposure of restricted data. A single delayed approval usually belongs in Medium or Low depending on its impact. Repeatedly submitting the same problem creates competing records and does not accelerate the response target.

Attachments and safe handling

Up to three PDF, PNG, JPEG, or text attachments are allowed, each no larger than 5 MB. Remove unrelated screen content and use the secure attachment classification for confidential records. Never upload passwords, tokens, or authenticator recovery material. TICKET-413 identifies a size breach; TICKET-415 identifies an unsupported attachment. If submission fails, retain the error code and retry once after removing the failing attachment.

Keep the conversation in one ticket

The ticket below reports that a closed attendance interval still shows zero after the five-minute calculation cycle. Useful additional evidence is the record reference and calculation status, rather than an entire payroll export. Replies should remain in the original ticket so the diagnosis and resolution form one history. Support can request missing information and pause the resolution investigation while awaiting it; first-response commitments remain as defined in Support Priority & SLA Policy.
