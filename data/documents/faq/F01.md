# Employee FAQ

## Which annual leave balance applies?
Contract A grants 25 days, B 15 days, and C 28 days per calendar year. Your available balance reflects monthly accrual and existing reservations. Contract B's entitlement is already part-time. Up to five approved carry-over days can be used until 31 March.

## Can I cancel approved leave?
Yes. In v2.0, open the request and choose Cancel leave before its first scheduled shift starts. This releases the balance immediately and notifies the manager. After leave starts, ask HR for review.

## How do I fix a missing punch?
Use My time > Attendance > Request correction within 14 calendar days while the payroll period is unlocked. Your manager must approve it. Recalculation runs within five minutes after approval. Locked or older records require HR Payroll review under Attendance Correction Guide v2.

## Can I arrange a shift swap?
Employees initiate swaps in v2.0 at least 24 hours before the earlier shift begins. The colleague must accept within 12 hours, then the manager must approve. Until approval, follow the original roster. Both employees must meet the team, skill, rest and weekly-hour checks.

## When can I see a payslip?
Published statements appear by 18:00 UTC on the last business day of the month. PAY-404 means no published statement exists. A missing allowance may reflect the cutoff on the 20th at 17:00 UTC.

## Who sees profile and sickness details?
Managers see business contact information and absence dates. Home address history belongs to authorized HR access, while medical attachments are restricted to HR absence administrators. Never put a medical attachment into an ordinary team message.
