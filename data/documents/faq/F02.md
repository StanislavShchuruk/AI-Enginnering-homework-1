# Manager FAQ

## What should I approve first?
Start with the oldest pending request in Team > Approvals. Decide within two business days. Reminders arrive after one business day; HR escalation follows after three. Support ticket SLAs are separate.

## Can I edit attendance directly?
In v2.0, decide the employee's correction request. Direct manager edits belong to v1.8. Locked records go to HR Payroll; do not remove a lock to clear the queue. Approved corrections recalculate within five minutes.

## Does colleague acceptance finalize a swap?
No. Check both assignments for skills, weekly hours and 11 consecutive hours of rest, then approve or reject. Until approval updates both assignments together, the original roster applies.

## Must I approve a leave cancellation?
Employees cancel pending or approved annual leave before its first scheduled shift starts. You receive a notification; no further approval is needed. Started leave requires HR review. The former instruction to contact HR Support is historical.

## Can a delegate approve everything?
A delegate acts only for the configured team and dates, without access to medical attachments or payslips. Self-approval is blocked and routed to an alternative approver. If APPROVAL-409 appears, reload: another decision has already been saved.

## Does more attendance mean automatic overtime?
No. Check prior approval and the actual-work interval. The 1.5 multiplier, 8-hour weekly overtime limit, 48-hour total-work limit and 40-hour TOIL cap still apply.
