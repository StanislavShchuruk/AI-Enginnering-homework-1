Employee Documents & E-Signatures

Document workspace

My documents separates employee uploads, assigned acknowledgements, and signature packages. Uploading a file does not mean HR has accepted it, and acknowledging a policy does not execute a contract amendment. Document status can be Uploaded, Under review, Accepted, Rejected, Awaiting signature, or Completed. A signature package must contain the approved terms before it is sent to the employee.

Upload and review

  1. Choose the correct document category before selecting a local file.
  2. Upload PDF, DOCX, PNG, or JPEG up to 10 MB; password-protected files are rejected.
  3. Confirm that the preview shows the intended pages and no unrelated material.
  4. Submit for review and inspect the status after the upload scan finishes.

DOC-413 indicates a size limit, DOC-415 an unsupported type, and DOC-422 a protected or unreadable file. Scanning normally finishes within two minutes. Do not rename an executable to bypass a type restriction. HR may reject an incomplete upload with a reason; submit a new revision rather than overwriting a previously accepted file.

Sign an assigned package

Open the package from My documents, read every page, and choose Continue to signature. Complete step-up MFA, confirm the displayed signer identity, and select Sign. The service records the package hash, timestamp in UTC, consent version, and signer display name. A completed receipt is available alongside the signed copy. Invitations expire after seven calendar days; HR must reissue an expired package rather than backdate a receipt.

If the package is wrong

If the signer identity or terms are wrong, stop before signing and choose Request correction. A signature cannot be removed through self-service; HR issues a replacement package and preserves the previous audit trail. Managers can see completion status for assigned team acknowledgements but cannot browse identity uploads. The receipt shows who signed, which package was signed, and when. Keep it with the completed package so HR can trace a later replacement.
