Overtime & Time-Off-in-Lieu Guide

Separate permission from recorded time

An attendance total above contracted hours does not automatically create payable overtime or TOIL. Obtain manager approval before the extra work starts. Contract A and C use a 40-hour week; Contract B uses a 24-hour week. Approved extra hours beyond the individual's contracted weekly hours qualify under Overtime & TOIL Policy. The weekly window runs Monday 00:00 through Sunday 23:59 in the workplace timezone.

Request extra work

  1. Open My time > Overtime and select the planned date and estimated duration.
  2. Describe the business reason and choose Cash or TOIL as the requested compensation.
  3. Submit at least one business day before the work begins.
  4. After approval, clock actual time using Clock-In / Clock-Out Manual and link the closed attendance interval.
  5. Review the finalized eligible duration before payroll cutoff.

Requests use 15-minute increments. Overtime is limited to 8 hours per week and total work must not exceed 48 hours in the same week. HR can decline an otherwise approved request if the final roster violates rest or total-hour controls. Unauthorized extra time remains visible as attendance but is flagged for review rather than silently deleted or automatically paid.

TOIL calculation and use

Approved overtime converts at 1.5 hours of TOIL per eligible hour. Two approved hours therefore create 3.00 hours of TOIL. The balance cap is 40 hours. A request that would exceed the cap must be split or changed to cash before approval. TOIL expires 90 calendar days after the overtime work date and cannot be carried forward by submitting a draft absence.

Reconciliation

To take TOIL, choose the TOIL absence type, enter hours in quarter-hour increments, and obtain manager approval. Approved usage deducts hours from the oldest unexpired credit first. Cash overtime uses 1.5 times the contract hourly base and rounds only the final currency line to two decimals. OT-422 identifies an invalid duration or limit; TOIL-410 identifies an expired credit. Corrections to the underlying attendance may require the overtime award to be recalculated, with the prior award retained in history.
