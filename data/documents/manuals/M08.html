Shift Schedule & Swap Guide

Read the published roster

Open My time > Schedule and choose Week or Month. Only Published shifts are binding; Draft shifts may still change. PeopleFlow displays start and end times in the workplace timezone. A shift ending after midnight shows its end date explicitly. Managers should publish the next week's roster by Thursday at 16:00 workplace time.

Propose an employee swap

From 2026-09-01, employees initiate swaps in v2.0. The earlier v1.8 manager-initiated workflow is described only in PeopleFlow Release Notes v1.8.

  1. Select a published shift and choose Propose swap.
  2. Choose an eligible colleague's published shift and review both resulting assignments.
  3. Submit at least 24 hours before the earlier of the two shift starts.
  4. Wait for the colleague to accept within 12 hours of the proposal.
  5. After acceptance, wait for manager approval before following the changed roster.

Acceptance by the colleague is not final authorization. The original assignments remain in force until the manager approves. A proposal expires after 12 hours without acceptance or when the earlier shift begins, whichever happens first. A withdrawn or rejected proposal does not modify either roster entry. An employee can withdraw before a manager decision.

Eligibility controls

Both employees must belong to the same scheduling team, hold the required shift skill tags, and have no overlapping assignment. The resulting schedule must preserve 11 consecutive hours of rest between shifts and stay within each employee's contracted weekly hours unless separately approved overtime exists. SHIFT-422 identifies a failed eligibility rule; SHIFT-409 identifies a shift already involved in a pending swap. Resolve the active proposal rather than creating duplicates.

Manager completion

Managers use Manager Approval Dashboard to compare both sides of the swap and record a reason if they reject it. Approval updates both assignments atomically and notifies both participants. If only one side appears changed, refresh once and submit a support incident instead of manually trading another shift. The swap does not transfer leave approvals, overtime approvals, or payroll allowances. Those records require their own checks when the new assignment changes eligibility.
