Clock-In / Clock-Out Manual

Register actual time

Clock-in and clock-out record actual attendance, not merely the published roster. Open My time > Clock and confirm the workplace timezone displayed above the controls. A shift may cross midnight; always inspect both the date and the time. PeopleFlow stores timestamps in UTC and presents them in the workplace timezone. A daylight-saving transition is calculated from elapsed UTC time, not by subtracting displayed local clock labels.

Daily procedure

  1. Select Clock in when work begins. Confirm that an open attendance session appears.
  2. Select Start break when an unpaid break begins, then End break on return.
  3. Select Clock out when work ends and review the closed attendance interval.
  4. Check the daily total after the next calculation cycle, which runs every five minutes.

One open attendance session is allowed per employee. CLOCK-409 means an open session already exists or a repeated clock-out was attempted. It does not mean the first event failed. Refresh the timeline before retrying. CLOCK-422 means event ordering is invalid, such as a break end preceding its start. PeopleFlow does not manufacture a missing clock-out from a roster end time.

Breaks and missing events

A 30-minute unpaid break is expected for shifts longer than six hours, but the system deducts recorded breaks only; it does not silently deduct an unrecorded break. A missing break generates a manager review flag. Worked time equals the closed interval minus recorded unpaid breaks. A 09:00-17:30 interval with a 30-minute unpaid break produces 8.00 hours.

Corrections and exceptions

For current records, use Attendance Correction Guide v2 to submit a self-service correction request. Employees propose corrected values; approval still belongs to the manager. A terminal outage should be documented with the approximate event times and a workplace explanation. Do not enter another clock-in to close an earlier session. If the total remains zero after five minutes despite a closed interval, use Attendance Troubleshooting and include the attendance calculation status in the ticket.
